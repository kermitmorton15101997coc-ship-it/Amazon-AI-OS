And we did make some invalid reports before, which is involved abuse conduct. Please check

out our plan of action below.

First, let's talk about what reports we've made that violate Amazon's rules and then

we'll summarize the reasons for our mistake.

Before, we found there another seller selling the product under our branded listings. At that

time, we asked the rights owner and learned that another seller did not obtain the

corresponding brand authorization. Then, we reported against the seller of selling

counterfeit goods because they used our brand “品牌名” without authorization.

Our mistakes:

1. We did not do the test buy of the product before reporting others selling counterfeit

goods.

2. We did not provide evidence of the sale of the counterfeit products, and did not

describe the details of the brand infringement. Therefore, our report is an invalid

report.

--Root reasons of the abuse conduct:

1. Our employees have never systematically learned Amazon's IPR infringement policy,

so we do not know what reports meets Amazon regulations.

This is the main reason for our previous invalid reports and not conducting test-buy.

2. Our company did not supervise our reporting behavior, which led to our operatingemployees to report the infringement of other sellers at will.

This is the reason for our employees to add other trademarks to listing. In addition, our

neglect of supervision previously led us to report infringement to the same seller repeatedly.

That's all the problems and the root cause of our violations.

-How we will use our report tool and our brand correctly in the future.

1. We will study Amazon's Reporting Infringement Policy from 2023.

(1) We will train all operation employees in the meeting room of the operations department

of the company. We will start to train employees on Amazon rules in June 2023 and we set

the training every Friday.

(2) These are the Amazon Reporting Infringement Policy that we have learned.

Policy violations are the result of a seller violating the activities listed on the Selling Policies

and Code of Conduct page. The correct steps to report violations are as follows.

① Go to Report Abuse

② Select violation type

③ Provide concerned Order ID or ASIN/ISBN, as applicable

④ Make sure to include the following information in the Please describe your issue field, as

applicable:

The store or business name of the seller we are reporting

The ASIN or ISBN of the item's detail page and the product title

The marketplaces for which the violation has occurred A concise explanation of the violation based on the policy

Supporting documentation (for example, order IDs, related messaging, or receipts)

⑤ Submit At present, all of our operating employees have learned these policies and know that we must strictly follow Amazon's policies.

(3) For the use of our reporting tool, our supervisor taught Amazon's requirements and

restrictions on sellers to use the tool to our operating employees. The main restrictions are

stated as follows.

① Sellers who have registered with the Amazon brand can use the "reporting violation tool"

to report suspected intellectual property rights, copyright and trademark infringement.

Sellers can log on to the https://brandregistry.amazon.com and choose [protection] -

[violation report]. If the details page of the product violates the Amazon policy, we can

submit a report through the [seller platform] - [account status] - [to report the abuse of the

Amazon policy].

② We need to make clear the type of infringement to be reported. There are three types of

infringement: trademark infringement, copyright infringement and patent infringement.

After we choose the type of infringement, we should submit all the required content,

otherwise we may be rejected by Amazon as an "incomplete report".

③ If reporting the trademark or patent infringement, we should complete the registration of

the trademark or patent in the country / region where the infringement is located. If the

seller only registered a trademark in the US site, reporting the ASIN from other sites is invalid.

Note that to report trademark infringement, we must first confirm that the details of the ASIN use our trademark.

④ Remember not to repeat the reports with audit results. We can log on to the Amazon

brand page, select "Brand Registration Customer Service Staff" and select " Report before

upgrading.

2. We plan conduct the assessment of the reporting policies and the intellectual

property policies of the right owners.

We will arrange the policy test after the training is completed. The test will be conducted in a

written test paper, with each person required to complete the 75 questions on the test paper,

with a full score of 150 points.

Employees with a test score above 140 pass the test. If an employee fails to pass the test, he

/ she needs to take the make-up test again and pass the second test, otherwise the

employee will not be able to continue to use the brand function in our account.

3. From now on, we will check every report before we do any report action.

(1) Our boss thinks that we should no longer arbitrarily use the reporting tools in our

account. From our experience this time, we have realized that this may pose the risk of

violating Amazon's policy. Therefore, our operations manager has decided to document the

specific details and report them to our boss before each report. Our boss needs to check the

type of violations and the information we fill in, and limit the time of reports to be made at

most once, in order to reduce the risk of violations. If we find that reporting may pose us a

risk of violation, our operations manager will promptly prevent this behavior.

(2) Our company has arranged for operations managers to supervise the reporting behavior

of our operating employees. This is a supervisory step for us to report violations.

① In the future, before our employees report the infringement and violations, our

operations manager needs to check whether the other party's account has violated our

intellectual property rights.

② If the other party does infringe, our manager needs to check the reasons for the

infringement, including using our copyright pictures, using our trademark, etc. Then, our

employees need to choose the violation for specific reasons. Our operations managers need

to supervise our employees to prevent our employees from choosing inappropriate causes

of infringement.

③ Finally, our employees need to report before our managers check that all the information

we submitted is correct and complete. We can only submit a report if there is no error.

And this is the new regulation we plan for employee behavior for reporting violations.

① We must control the frequency of using the reporting tool, not more than 8 times a

month.

② Employees are prohibited from repeatedly reporting against the same ASIN or account. If

our report is not accepted by Amazon, we need to meet to analyze the reason and correct it.

Any employee is prohibited from repeating the reporting after it is not accepted.

③ The most important thing is that we prohibit employees from submitting false statements

of rights. Our employees must ensure that they understand our intellectual property rights

and submit the correct report content.The above is our new action plan. We will prevent similar mistakes from happening again.

Now，the attorney of the brand has been changed and we will use the brand under Amazon

policy. Please help us reinstate this brand “品牌名” in our account brand registry. Thanks so

much.

Yours,
