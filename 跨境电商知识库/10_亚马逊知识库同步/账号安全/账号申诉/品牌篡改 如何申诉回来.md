品牌篡改   如何申诉回来？

到品牌支持里面开case内容如下

Dear Amazon Seller Support, We have listed ASIN: B000DJDJBT on 24th May, 2021, and everything went alright before, but on recent days, the brand for this ASINs have been changed by some seller maliciously lead to the detail pages showing the wrong brand KAFOT (恶搞品牌）and the edit page of ASIN: B000DJDJBT reminds that &quot;You need approval to list in this brand&quot;. Obviously, What they did has caused huge impact for us. I believe they didn't sell any item for this ASIN and their act of changing brand casually is agaist Amazon Seller Policy about the Product Detail Page. In order to avoid unnecessary troubles caused for customers during the shopping process, please help change back to my registered brand: ABCDERT Please help us complete the request. Thank you.

如果之前自己的品牌是白名单品牌，那么上述case内容先请求改为白名单品牌，然后改成功后第一时间用表格刷到新的品牌（R标）
