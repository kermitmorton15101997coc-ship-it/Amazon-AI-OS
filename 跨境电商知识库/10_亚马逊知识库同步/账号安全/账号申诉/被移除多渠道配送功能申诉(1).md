Dear Amazon,

Thank you for informing us that our removal order privileges were temporarily blocked on 05/23/2022 due to a potential removal order policy violation. I am very sorry and regretful for violating Amazon policy. We immediately investigated the cause, I would like to share with you my plan of Action on addressing the issue.

• What was the root cause that led to the issue?

I swear all of the removal orders were not be sent as customer orders, gifts, or promotional items or other different platform orders. We just used removal tools to send out the product for replacement order to our customer, because the products was lost by logistic company or broken. But we don't know this was violated the policy. We don't plan to sell this product in future, most of them had been disposed now.


• What actions will you take to resolve the issue?

We had know that we shouldn't use the removal tools to send out the product to the customer as a replacement order. We promise never use the removal tool to send out the product to the private address again.
We will send all the product out via Amazon, if we need send our customer for a replacement in future, we will use Amazon multi-channel fulfillment order.

• What steps will you take to prevent future issues?
1)Our company had a meeting with all the employee to learn the policy of Amazon carefully.
2)We will teach all the new employees to learn more carefully about the policy before they start to work. This behavior will not be repeated in the future.
3)Regularly learn new Amazon policies to ensure daily operations comply with Amazon rules.
4)we understand that providing quality products and excellent services is what it takes to be a good seller, we are devoting ourselves to reaching 100% satisfaction and take full responsibilities for our every customer.
5)Training the customer service of our employee to make them more professional, and do not use the removal tool to send out the product again anymore .

Please give us a chance to correct our mistake ,we will try our best to be a good seller. Amazon is a nice company so we will try our best to be a nice seller.

Thank you so much for your help and guide, please kindly advice if you need more information, thank you so much and your kindly.
Best wishes,
