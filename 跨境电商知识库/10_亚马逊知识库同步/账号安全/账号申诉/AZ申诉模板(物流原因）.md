We are contacting you regarding an A-to-z Guarantee claim.

The order XXXXX was shipped on XXXX with tracking number XXXXXX by carrier XXXXX and the delivery date was XXXXXX. 【The buyer sent a message to us on XXXXX and said she never received the package and was hoping she could have a new one . We replied her soon and told her the tracking information shows that the package has been received,maybe she could contact XXX for pick up, and we also provide either refund or a replacement for her to choose. But there was no reply after that until we received the A-to-Z Guarantee claim. 】(根据实际情况阐述）

We tried our best to provide the satisfied service for the customer, but the bad logistic service made a bad experience. Now we've lost our product, and the payment has been returned to the buyer.

We just hope that you can review the result of this order again and eliminate the impact of this order against our ODR.

Steps we have taken and will continue to take:

1.We have contacted with our logistics company and ensure to offer a better service.

2.We will use FBA to fulfill most of our orders.

Thank you!
