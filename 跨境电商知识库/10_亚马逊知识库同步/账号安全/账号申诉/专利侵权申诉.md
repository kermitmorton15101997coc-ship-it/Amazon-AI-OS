## 第 1 页

We regret to have received a notification of infringement of a patent by us. For this, our
company must sincerely apologize.
At this point, we must honestly admit our mistake and would like to explain it. Honestly, we
have not been Amazon sellers for long and are still in the stage of learning Amazon's procedures
and policies. More importantly, due to our limited level in uploading the product 你的 asin, we
did not bother to look for information about the patent aspect, including the patent number, and
we did not realize that the product with patent number 6181241 was at risk of infringement, and
thus made an embarrassing mistake. In fact, our product is different from the patented product in
both design and function, so please believe that we did not intend to infringe the patented IPR,
but made this mistake unintentionally.
After we received the notification, we immediately checked our link and wanted to remove
our ASIN, hoping to minimize the damage to them. And we found that 你的 asin had been
promptly removed from Amazon. In addition, with the professional help of our IP firm, we
thoroughly reviewed all the products we currently sell to make sure there were no other Amazon
violations. (We would like to let you know that one of our solutions is to type out all titles and
descriptions below this website to ensure that our products do not violate any other IP products.
https://register.dpma.de/DPMAregister/pat/basis)
To be a qualified Amazon seller, we have analyzed this incident so that we can summarize
some actions for our future operation. First, we will definitely improve the language level and IP
infringement detection ability of all our members to avoid this situation. Second, we will set up
regular training to help our employees better understand Amazon's rules and raise their
awareness of compliance. Fourth, we will require that all ASINS be submitted to a team of IP
consultants for final review before a product can be listed. Going forward, we will strictly enforce
double-checking of product information.
We will definitely fight together with Amazon to protect intellectual property and reject any
infringement. We can assure you that this will not happen in the future.
