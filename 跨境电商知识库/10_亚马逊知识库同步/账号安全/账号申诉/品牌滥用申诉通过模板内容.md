-- Policies for editing detail pages:
① You may only update detail pages to better or more accurately describe the product
as originally listed. For example, allowed updates include additional details,
descriptions, grammar fixes, or removal of content that violates Amazon's policies.
② You may not update existing product information if a new version of the product is
available. This includes changes to colors, sizes, materials, properties and trade names.
Instead, create new product detail pages for each new version. Case in point:
Manufacturers have introduced a brand new remote with four buttons, replacing the
two with two, to update their streaming media players. This item is significantly
different from the previous version and must be posted as a new ASIN.
③ If you choose to rebrand your product, you must create a new ASIN instead of
updating an existing ASIN. This is the case even if the item is unbranded and not
significantly changed after the brand change. This includes adding branding to
items that were not previously branded.
