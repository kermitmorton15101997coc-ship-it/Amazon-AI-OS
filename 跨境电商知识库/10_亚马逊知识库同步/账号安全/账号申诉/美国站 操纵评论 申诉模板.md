-- The steps that we have taken to eliminate the manipulation of product reviews and how these measures will prevent future violations

We have deleted the third party from our WeChat. On March 12, our manager hold a meeting to discuss this issue. He said anyone who has added a third party, whether or not we have contacted the third party to manipulate reviews, we must delete the third party as soon as possible. Everyone who attended the meeting signed up a file promising not to contact any third party.

Our manager has led us to learn Amazon customer product reviews policies.

We learned that Amazon's Community Guidelines have specific policies that are meant to protect the authenticity of Customer Reviews, and we must comply with these policies.

Customer reviews are an integral part of the customer shopping experience on Amazon. Customers use these reviews to learn more about the product, and customer reviews also help sellers understand the customers’ sentiment about their products.

If we do the following things, we will violate customer reviews policies. They are:

- A seller posts a review of their own product or their competitor's product.

- A seller offers a third party a financial reward, discount, free products, or other compensation in exchange for a review.

- A seller offers to provide a refund or reimbursement after the buyer writes a review.

- A seller asks a reviewer to change or remove their review.

- A seller inserts a request for a positive Amazon review or an incentive in exchange for a review into product packaging or shipping box.

- A seller uses a customer account to write or change a review on his or his competitor’s product.

We will do the following things to prevent manipulating reviews.

We have strengthen reviews policies training for employees. We have set up a WeChat group to summarize and discuss our confusion and ideas. In the future, new employees will receive more comprehensive training of reviews policies before they start working.

We will never contact any third party by any way, such as WeChat, QQ, Google, Facebook, etc.

We will monitor our employees’ activities. Using a buyer account to post reviews is not allowed. Asking their family member or friends to do such things is also prohibited. Any infractions are found, the employee will be imposed heavy fines, demotions and even dismissal.

We will sell high-quality products on Amazon and improve our service quality. If customers are satisfied with our products and services, we believe they will post positive reviews.

We will never offer to provide payment (including money or gift cards), refund or reimbursement, free product, discounts on future purchases or other gifts to exchange for positive reviews.

We have totally realized that we made a big mistake. Hope you could give us one more chance. We would appreciate it if you could reactivate our account.
