And this just help us to get the Amazon Buybox.Thanks to the professional Amazon operator, the training courses and the Amazon Buybox, our Amazon.de business got a qualitative leap, and this just helped us to get more orders these days.

2. Regarding effective tracking and delivery date:

a) Regarding order fulfillment, we guarantee that all orders will be shipped and refunded in case of cancellation. We actually do it according to the customer's request. You can check the past account emails with the customer.

b) More than 90% of our orders are sent by YunExpress, and all tracking information can be traced back. So far, all orders have been successfully delivered. The order information in the attached table is for reference.

3. Regarding the buyer's feedback:

In the past 30 days, we have received 0 feedback, because our order volume is very small, in March 2021 Amazon.de a total of 16 orders. And we all know that there will be no more feedback due to the expiration of the review time, so we take the following measures.

a) Since our current feedback is still not enough to support our sales, we have taken steps to click the "request for review" button to invite our customers to provide us with fair feedback.

b) For orders that have been shipped, we proactively contact buyers and confirm whether they have successfully received the package. For parcels with damaged logistics, we proactively provide customers with partial refunds or re-send gifts as compensation. The mail can show that we have performed our duties in time.

Part 2: The actions we have taken to resolve this issue

1. We fulfilled all orders within required date and prepared the order information with tracking information for your team review.

2. Our customer services are actively handling customers' complaints and consultation and tried our best to provide a good shopping experience to customers, we have compensated customers timely to ensure their complaint has been handled well.

3.We also made sure each employee knew Amazon rules of Velocity Limits and Account Reviews clearly before we writing our action plan. On 20 March, each employee have attend a meeting hold by our boss to address this issue and ensure everyone understand the rule.

Part 3: The steps we have taken to prevent such issue

1. A good after-sale department matters. So timely and efficient response to the inquiries of customers should be made, we believe adequate communication will surely increase the level of satisfaction. We have demanded our after-sale staffs to respond customers more quickly and proactively to any problems with customer orders to keep our customers more informed and help prevent a similar issue from happening. Providing better customer services is another important part we need to consider:

a)Dealing with customers dissatisfaction or complaints more proactively, making sincere apology first and then investigate the situation to take corresponding steps.

b)Frequently reviewing our communications from buyers to better understand their concerns.

c)For customers who are not satisfied with our products or service, we will try our best to solve their problems by offering free replacement or initiating a full refund at their choice.

d)Pay attention to all the negative messages, buyer complaints, negative reviews, and return requests to find out what goes wrong and give the reasonable solutions.

e)Clearly indicate return and refund policies, streamline and simplify complex returns processes wherever possible, and make sure customers can receive the refund within 48 hours after we receiving the returned goods.

2. We also need to improve our shipping service for customers to satisfy our customers, which is also another indirect way to help buyers give feedback about our service in account. Our warehouse keepers should keep the follows in mind to make sure timely and correct delivery and make customer satisfied:

1) The delivery order given by the sales department must be completely packaged and sealed on the same day without special reasons.

2) Ensure that the products prepared for shipment are in line with customer requirements. Ensure ship items to the right address provided by Amazon.

3）Frequently help to check the status of buyers’ orders during the shipment.

4）Remember to follow up the order in time according to the tracking number until the package is to be delivered successfully.

5) Remember to notify buyers immediately of any delay or interrupted tracking information.

6) Contact the buyer in advance to let him know the shipping status to timely avoid sending to the wrong address.

3. Check the order daily for timely delivery. Our manager has summoned up a group meeting to discuss about how to quickly and effectively deal with our orders. She emphasized that it is important to ensure that the order can be dispatched in time.

a)We are required to report the delivery of orders twice a week.

b)For orders that cannot be delivered due to the Logistics restrictions, pass to customer service to communicate with the buyer in 24 hours after noticing the abnormal.

c)After obtaining the seller's consent, initiate full refund.

d)As orders of our Amazon.de becomes more and more, we are now planning to send our goods to Amazon.de FBA, hope Amazon can help to supervise and control our goods quality, shipping services and after service.

We are a not experienced seller on Amazon, we are still lacking in the experience for managing our seller account on Amazon. But we know that orders on our account must be authentic that come from our real customers. Therefore, we can 100% guarantee that these orders placed on our account are really authentic. In the later work, we must strictly abide by your Amazon’s policies so as to be a qualified seller on Amazon.
