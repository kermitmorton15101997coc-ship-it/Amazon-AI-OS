## 第 2 页

Part 2： 申诉 tips 
移除差评的条件（自查是否存在这些情况，写申诉的时候往这个上面靠）： 
1. 非 VP 评论 
2. 客户 Profile 全部是差评 
3. 客户 Profile 隐藏评论 
4. 客户同一个时间留下多条评论 
5. 客户的评论是正向反馈，星级是 1 星 
6. 客户 Profile 间隔多年留评 
7. 客户购买同类型产品留差评 
8. 客户所描述的内容与产品严重不符 
9. 客户 profile 的差评占全部评论的比重大 
10. 客户昵称为评论内容，昵称异常 
11. 客户评论不一样的产品但是评论标题都相似的 
 
一般申诉写法参考： 
1. The user never contacted us about the product and directly left us a bad review. Obviously, a competitor 
intentionally left us a negative review to lower our product rating. 
2. This user does not have a profile picture, and even does not display the content of bad reviews, which is 
obviously a malicious review of the illegal account 
3.no personal photos, no account description. 
 
Key Submission Guidelines: 
• DO NOT submit multiple requests for the same ASIN and abuse type
