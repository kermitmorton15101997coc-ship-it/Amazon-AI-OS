网红开发信模版

邀约信成功率不高如何解决？

一般来说回复率大概是10%-20%左右，大家可以自行判断一下

影响因素主要有以下两种：
1. 技术原因
解释：比如邮箱不是公司邮箱影响可信度，注册的邮箱被系统误判为垃圾邮件，产品不够好等等
2. 技巧原因
解释：比如措辞不够得当，没有明确的call to action等等。几个比较重要的准则：
- 不要直接复制粘贴，一定要根据达人本身来写
- 多用emoji，不要让对方觉得你是广告/机器
- 邮件内容要简洁清晰，不要期望一封邮件就能解决所有后续沟通的事情，邀约信只需要解决邀约的问题就可以了‼️
- 可能的话可以用当地语言写邮件，这个亲测有效，之前尝试用法语触达了一批，成功率大概在50-60%，远远比英语的时候高

那么基于上述几个准则，有哪些板块是我们可以优化的呢：

邮件标题：写清楚你是谁+这封邮件目的是什么，同时也不要忘记加一些emoji

参考例子：
- Collab Opportunity ：Top beauty brand xx
- Inquiry about brand placement for xx😊
- Urgent‼️Paid Post for xx💗

邮件正文：介绍你找他的目的+吸引点+CTA

参考例子：
Subject: Exciting Collaboration Opportunity: Showcase Our High-Quality Wigs to Your Stylish Audience

Dear [网红的名字],

I am Ana, owner of a reputable wig store specializing in high-quality products. Your impeccable style and strong connection with your followers have caught my attention. Let's collaborate and introduce our transformative wigs to your dedicated audience.

As a partner, we offer:

Product Integration: Choose from our high-quality wigs that match your style. Showcase them in your content through posts, videos, or any format you prefer.

Honest Reviews: Your genuine feedback is valued. Share your thoughts on the quality, versatility, and impact our wigs have on your style.

Promotional Efforts: Create engaging content featuring our wigs, highlighting different hairstyles and their transformative effect on your look.

Compensation: We provide competitive compensation for your efforts in promoting our wigs to your engaged audience.

Let's discuss the details and timeline for our collaboration. Your influence and style make you an ideal ambassador for our high-quality wig collection.

Looking forward to hearing from you soon.

Warmest regards,

Ana

[公司名称] Owner
