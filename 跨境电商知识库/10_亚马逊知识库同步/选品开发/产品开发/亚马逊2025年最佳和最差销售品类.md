The categories in this report were 
assessed based on four core criteria:
Profitability
Consumer Demand
Cost & Competition
Market Saturation
Categories with high margins, strong average sale prices, and consistent 
revenue growth offer the greatest opportunities for sustainable success.
Data used to assess the profitability of each category includes responses from Jungle Scout’s 2025 State of 
the Amazon Seller survey, in which more than 1,000 brands were asked about average margins and year-over-
year profitability fluctuations on Amazon.
Data used to assess consumer demand in each category includes responses from Jungle Scout’s 2025 
Consumer Trends survey, in which approximately 1,000 U.S. consumers were asked about anticipated 
spending habits in 2025, as well as sales data from Amazon Category Insights and Jungle Scout Cloud.
Data used to assess market saturation in each category includes responses from Jungle Scout’s 2025 State 
of the Amazon Seller survey, in which more than 1,000 brands were asked to identify their primary selling 
category on Amazon, as well as sub-category level insights from Jungle Scout Cobalt.
Data used to assess the advertising costs of each category includes responses from Jungle Scout’s 2025 State 
of the Amazon Seller survey, in which more than 1,000 brands were asked about average cost-per-click in their 
primary product categories, as well as Jungle Scout Cobalt data from 10k+ PPC campaigns.
Categories with rising consumer interest and stable year-over-year sales 
growth signal strong potential for long-term success.
High cost-per-click (CPC) and saturated markets can make it difficult to 
compete, while categories with lower ad costs and reasonable competition 
generate higher ROI.
Fewer competing sellers means more opportunities to capture market 
share, whereas oversaturated categories make it hard to stand out.

COMPETITIVE STRATEGIES FOR APPLIANCE BRANDS
For premium appliances ($100+), set higher 
ACoS targets and go after high-intent, high-ROI 
placements such as Sponsored Brands Video 
and DSP retargeting campaigns. For lower-cost

maximize reach and drive recurring purchases.

understanding of product dimensions, materials,

justifying price points.

seek extended warranties, service plans, and 
support, which can be upsold post-purchase 
through Amazon’s after-sale messaging. 
Integrating warranty upsells increases 
repeat purchases and customer retention.

products, you can also capture first-party data for

COMPETITIVE STRATEGIES FOR PATIO, LAWN & GARDEN BRANDS
Spring demand spikes require early planning 
to maximize profitability. Stock inventory and 
launch advertising campaigns 4-6 weeks before 
the peak spring season to avoid lost sales. 
Gradual price increases during peak months can 
also help maximize profit margins.
Post-summer sales dips can be offset by 
Prime Day promotions, while winter decor, 
heating accessories, and off-season garden 
care products can drive end-of-year sales. Run 
clearance or BOGO promotions in late summer 
to move excess inventory before seasonal 
demand dips.
Complementary products, such as gardening 
tools bundled with soil kits or patio furniture 
bundled with covers, boost order values and 
appeal to seasonal shoppers prioritizing multiple 
products at once. Subscription-based purchases 
(like recurring fertilizer deliveries) can help create 
longer-term revenue streams that generate sales 
throughout the season.

COMPETITIVE STRATEGIES FOR AUTOMOTIVE BRANDS
Many shoppers search for branded auto parts 
(e.g., “Ford brake pads,”), making it difficult 
for competitors to rank. Use search query 
reports – from Amazon or from more robust 
tools like Jungle Scout Cobalt – to find and 
target unbranded, high-intent search terms 
(e.g., “high-performance brake pads for trucks”) 
and capture organic search share. Investing 
in detailed comparison charts in A+ Content 
can also improve conversions by highlighting 
how aftermarket parts match or exceed 
manufacturer brands.
Many automotive products lack repeat purchase 
urgency since consumers tend to buy only when 
something breaks. You can encourage repeat 
purchases by using time-based retargeting 
to offer discounts when customers are likely 
to need a replacement (e.g., a customer who 
buys windshield wipers gets a reminder email 
in 6 months with a 15% off offer), or by offering 
discounted add-ons directly after purchase (e.g., 
a battery charger to a customer who bought a 
car battery).
Many auto parts shoppers on Amazon aren’t 
experts – they are DIYers or people looking for 
mechanics to install parts and accessories for 
them. You can improve conversions on relevant 
products by building listings that provide 
detailed information instantly – like clearly stating 
which vehicle model your products fit and 
creating video walkthroughs for installation (e.g., 
a short, mobile-friendly video showing how to 
install your brand’s car battery).
